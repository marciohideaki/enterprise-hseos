The campaign scripts below were committed as run, but the repository lint gate (zero warnings, unicorn rules) was not part of the commit hook that checked them, and CI failed on them. They were then fixed with eslint --fix plus a few manual edits, and formatted with prettier:
  pre-w6-c1/run-c1.cjs, pre-w6-c2/run-c2.cjs, pre-w6-c3/run-c3.cjs, pre-w6-c3/diag-init-no-cost.mjs, pre-w6-c3/diag-paid.mjs, pre-w6-c3-retry/diag-precedence.mjs,
  pre-w6-c5-attempt1/run-c5.cjs, pre-w6-c5-attempt1/repro-offline-stream-shape.cjs, pre-w6-c7/run-c7.cjs, pre-w6-c8/task.cjs, pre-w6-c11/handshake.cjs, pre-w6-c9/*.cjs
The edits are behaviour-preserving by kind: replaceAll for global replacements, catch parameter renames, comments inside empty catch blocks, removal of unused variables and of one unused intermediate value, toReversed for a one-off cleanup list, arrow wrappers instead of passing JSON.parse or sha straight to map, and handshake.cjs sets process.exitCode instead of calling process.exit.
These scripts were run on paid or quota-bound accounts and cannot be repeated for an equivalence check; the copies that ran live outside the repository under /build/tmp/wc/cN and are removed in the cleanup. Only the C9 scripts have an equivalence note (pre-w6-c9/script-equivalence.txt), because they have a free mock path. Every receipt keeps the label it had.
